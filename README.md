1. **Download**:
   - Tap the **[Download APK Button](https://github.com/manas-gitspace/Work-Scheduler-Memorii/releases/latest/download/app-debug.apk)** on your phone, or scan the QR code above.
2. **Open File**:
   - Once downloaded (~18.6 MB), tap **Open** or find `app-debug.apk` in your **Downloads** folder.
3. **Allow Unknown Apps (First-time prompt)**:
   - If Android prompts: *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
     - Tap **Settings**
     - Enable **Allow from this source** (or *Install unknown apps*)
4. **Install**:
   - Tap **Install** and then **Open** to launch **Memorii**!

## ✨ App Highlights
- **Smart Task Prioritization**: Organize routines by morning, afternoon, evening, and night.
- **Interactive Calendar & Timelines**: Seamless scheduling across dates.
- **Alarm System**: Full-screen alarm overlays for critical tasks with exact alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`).
- **Home Screen Widgets**: Quick-access interactive widgets in 1x2, 2x2, and 4x4 formats.
- **Custom Themes**: Dynamic dark mode and glassmorphism styling.
